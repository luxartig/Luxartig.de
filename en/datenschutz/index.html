2. Hosting and Server Log Files

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically collects technical information transmitted by your browser: IP address, date and time of the request, page accessed, browser used and operating system. This data is processed exclusively to technically operate and secure the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and is not merged with other data sources.

3. Contact Form

When you use our contact form, your enquiry is either processed directly server-side and forwarded to us by email, or your email application opens with a pre-filled message to info@luxartig.de, depending on the technical configuration. In either case, your details are not stored permanently on a third-party server. Processing your details serves to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Embedded Fonts

This website loads the typefaces "Inter", "Inter Tight" and "Space Grotesk" from Google Fonts, directly from the provider's servers. In doing so, your IP address is transmitted to Google so the font files can be delivered. We are not aware of any further storage. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent typeface (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Embedded Software Library

For the animations on this website, we load the "GSAP" library via the content delivery network cdnjs (Cloudflare, Inc.). In doing so, your IP address is transmitted to Cloudflare so the file can be delivered. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in performant delivery of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Cookies and Consent Management

On your first visit to this website, a cookie banner appears where you can choose whether to allow only technically necessary functions or, in addition, external services (see point 7). Your choice is stored exclusively locally in your browser (localStorage), not on our servers, and can be changed at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer. This storage is technically necessary so that your decision is not requested again on every page visit (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, § 25(2) No. 2 TTDSG). Without your consent, this website does not set cookies for analytics or marketing purposes.

7. Chat Widget and Appointment Booking

For live chat and online appointment booking, we use the services of an external provider for scheduling and customer communication. These only load after you select "Accept all" in the cookie banner, or actively reveal the calendar via the "Load Calendar" button. Doing so sets cookies and transmits your IP address to the provider in order to provide the chat or calendar view. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

8. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to request information about the personal data stored about you, as well as to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing. Simply contact info@luxartig.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
