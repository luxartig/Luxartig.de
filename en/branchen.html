Solutions for your industry

Every industry has its own everyday challenges. We know them from practice, and have the right digital solution for each one.

Trades

Your Challenges
  • Barely any time for a website and online marketing alongside day-to-day work
  • Missed calls while you're on site or under a car
  • Outdated or no online presence at all: customers can't find you
  • Scheduling appointments eats up needless time on the phone
How Luxartig Helps

We build you a website that's easy to maintain yourself, set up a central inbox that bundles every enquiry, and automate appointment reminders, so fewer jobs fall through the cracks.

  • More online visibility, including with customers who don't know you yet
  • Fewer missed calls thanks to a central inbox
  • Automated appointment reminders instead of phone tag

Hospitality

Your Challenges
  • The menu online is often outdated: wrong prices or opening hours
  • Reservations only by phone, hard to reach exactly during rush hour
  • Social media presence falls behind in the day-to-day stress
  • Hard to stand out from the competition online
How Luxartig Helps

With a self-maintainable website system, automated online reservations and an actively managed social media presence, we make sure your venue feels just as inviting online as it does in person.

  • Reservations run automatically, even outside opening hours
  • Menu and opening hours always up to date, edited in minutes
  • A strong, well-kept social media presence instead of an abandoned profile
