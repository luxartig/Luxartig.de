Fewer Missed Calls: Automation for Trades Businesses

6 min read · Luxartig Editorial
← Back to Blog

You're up a ladder, under a sink, or in the middle of an install, and the phone rings. Of course you can't get it. The problem: the caller often just hangs up and rings the next business instead. Every missed call is a possible job, lost.

The Real Problem: Too Many Channels, Too Little Time

Calls, WhatsApp messages, emails, maybe an enquiry on Instagram too. As a one-person or small business, you're meant to keep an eye on all of it alongside the actual work. That's barely achievable in practice, and that's exactly where automation comes in: not to replace you, but to keep your back covered.

What a Central Inbox Takes Off Your Hands

  • Every enquiry in one place: email, forms and messages come together instead of scattered across five apps
  • Online appointment calendar: customers book their own free slot without you having to call back
  • Automated reminders: fewer missed appointments, less dead time in the calendar

At the next stage, the Full AI level, an AI assistant automatically answers common questions (about opening hours, prices or availability, for example) and only gets in touch with you when it's actually necessary.

How Much Time You Really Gain

A conservative estimate: five missed calls a week, half of which would have become a job. Over a year, that's over 100 possible jobs, lost simply because nobody could pick up. Automation costs you from €15 per month for inbox and calendar. In most cases, the math clearly works in your favour.
