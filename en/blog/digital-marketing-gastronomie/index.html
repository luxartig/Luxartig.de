Digital Visibility for Hospitality: Menu, Bookings, Reach

6 min read · Luxartig Editorial
← Back to Blog

A guest spontaneously searches for a restaurant nearby one evening. They find you, but the menu online is from last year, the opening hours are wrong, and a booking is only possible by phone, while nobody's answering right now. The guest goes elsewhere. It doesn't have to be that way.

1. The Menu Has to Always Be Current

Nothing looks less credible than an outdated menu with wrong prices, or dishes that don't exist anymore. With a website system you can maintain yourself, you update the menu, opening hours and specials in a few minutes, with no need to call a web designer.

2. Bookings Automatically, Instead of by Phone

Right at rush hour, the phone is busy: with guests in the venue, not with calls. An online booking calendar takes reservations while you're serving. Automated reminders also make sure fewer reserved tables stay empty.

3. Social Media as a Daily Shop Window

A photo of today's special, a story from a packed restaurant on a Friday night: that's often exactly what decides where guests go that evening. With a cleanly set up social media account and a well-kept Google Business listing, you're present exactly where your guests are already searching and scrolling.

  • Menu & opening hours, self-maintainable via the website system
  • Bookings via the online calendar, even outside opening hours
  • Google Business listing with current photos and reviews
  • A social media presence that shows what's happening at your place right now
