Why a Fixed Point of Contact Beats a Ticket System

4 min read · Aladin Almadhoun
← Back to Blog

I've experienced myself how frustrating it is to send a support request to some anonymous address and wait for days for an answer from whoever happens to pick it up, someone who doesn't know your business at all. That's why I do things deliberately differently at Luxartig.

What "Personal Support" Actually Means With Us

When you become a Luxartig client, you get me as your fixed point of contact, not a rotating support team that has to be told from scratch each time what your business does. I know your business, your industry and your goals, and that alone saves you time and nerves on every single enquiry.

  • Direct contact by phone or email: no hold queue, no ticket system
  • Explanations without jargon, understandable for non-technical people
  • Personal support, reachable in person
  • Usually a reply within 24 hours on business days

Why This Matters Especially for Small Businesses

Large agencies are built for large clients with their own marketing department. As a trades business or restaurant, you're often just one number among many there. At Luxartig, it's the other way round: I deliberately work with a manageable number of clients, so I can genuinely do right by every single business, explained clearly, at a predictable monthly price, with no sales pressure.
