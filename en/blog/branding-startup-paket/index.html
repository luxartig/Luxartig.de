From Logo to Business Card: What's in the Standard Package

5 min read · Luxartig Editorial
← Back to Blog

A customer calls because they found your business card at last month's trade fair. They quickly check your Instagram profile. Then they see the stamp on your quote. Does it all feel like one and the same company, or like three different ones? That's exactly what decides whether they trust you, before you've even spoken.

Why Consistency Matters More Than an Expensive Logo

Many small businesses have a logo that was thrown together ten years ago, business cards from the copy shop, and a flyer in a completely different typeface. Each piece is fine on its own. Together, it looks unprofessional. Customers pick up on that unconsciously, before they can even judge your prices or quality.

That's exactly where our Standard Package comes in: logo design, stamp, flyer and business card are created together, in one consistent visual language, including a website system and your own email address. At a fair price, you get a complete, coherent presence.

What's Included, Exactly

  • Logo Design: your mark, tailored to your industry and audience
  • Stamp: for invoices, delivery notes and quotes
  • Flyer: for displays, trade fairs or mailings
  • Business Card: your presence in a face-to-face conversation
  • Website System & Email Address: so your digital presence is just as coherent

The Next Step: Getting Seen

Consistent branding is the foundation. It only becomes visible once customers can find you too. That's why our VIP Package builds directly on this and adds a Google Business listing and social media setup. Read more in the article Google Business & Social Media: How to Get Found in Passau.
