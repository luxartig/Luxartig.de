Google Business & Social Media: How to Get Found in Passau

7 min read · Luxartig Editorial
← Back to Blog

"But we already have a website." True, but a website alone doesn't make people find you. Most local searches today go through Google Maps and the Google Business listing, not classic Google search. If you're missing there, or incomplete, you simply get overlooked.

The Google Business Listing: Your Digital Shop Window

The listing shows opening hours, photos, reviews and directions to you: right there in the search, without anyone needing to visit your website. For trades businesses and restaurants, this is often the very first point of contact. A complete, well-kept listing with real photos and current opening hours makes the decisive difference between "gets called" and "gets skipped".

Social Media: Trust Before the First Contact

Before someone calls or drops by, they often take a look at Instagram or Facebook first. A current, well-kept profile signals: someone who knows their trade and is active works here. An empty or abandoned profile, on the other hand, feels like a shop with the shutters down, even if you're actually fully booked.

  • Google Business listing with complete details, photos and category
  • Social media account setup with a consistent profile picture and description
  • First posts that show who you are and what you do

Together They Work Stronger

Your Google Business listing and social media profiles link to each other and to your website: that raises visibility at every single touchpoint.
