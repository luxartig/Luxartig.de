About Luxartig

In short: Luxartig (Luxartig, Digital & Print) is run by Aladin Almadhoun and based in Salzweg near Passau, Germany. We are the digital all-in-one partner for trades and hospitality businesses in and around Passau: design, social media, website and online shop, digital business card and automation from a single source.

Where we come from

Before we built websites, we stood in kitchens and on building sites ourselves. We know the Saturday night when three parties want to book a table at once, and the call that comes in while you're under the sink. Our solutions grew out of exactly these situations, not off a drawing board.

What we do for you

  • Design & Branding: logo, flyer, business card and stamp
  • Social Media Management: posts, reels, content calendar and community
  • Website & Online Shop: hosting, domain, SEO and GEO included (visibility in Google and in AI answers)
  • Digital Business Card: QR code and NFC
  • Automation: chat, calendar, inbox and follow-up

How we work

  • One fixed point of contact who knows your business
  • Clear explanations, no jargon
  • Usually a reply within 24 hours on business days
  • A free, no-obligation initial consultation
