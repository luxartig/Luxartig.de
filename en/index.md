# Luxartig: Design, Website, Social Media & Automation in Passau

> Luxartig is the digital all-in-one partner for trades and hospitality businesses in and around Passau, Germany. Personal support, explained in plain language, with one fixed point of contact.

## Services

- **Design & Branding:** logo, flyer, business card, stamp
- **Social Media Management:** posts, reels, content calendar, community
- **Website & Online Shop:** hosting, domain, SEO, GEO (visibility in Google and in AI answers such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude)
- **Digital Business Card:** QR code, NFC
- **Automation:** chat, calendar, inbox, follow-up

## Why Luxartig

We have stood in kitchens and on building sites ourselves. Our solutions grew out of real situations: bookings run automatically, enquiries from phone, WhatsApp and Instagram land in one inbox, appointments confirm themselves. Usually a reply within 24 hours on business days. The initial consultation is free and without obligation.

## FAQ

- **Do I need to be tech-savvy?** No. We explain everything in plain language and handle the technical setup completely.
- **How long until my website goes live?** Usually 1–3 weeks, once your text, images and logo are ready.
- **Can I book individual services?** Yes, start with a single service and add more at any time.
- **What is a digital business card?** A contact page with a QR code or NFC chip that can be saved to a phone with one tap.
- **What does automation get me?** Enquiries get answered, appointments get booked and all messages land in one inbox.
- **How does working together look?** A free initial consultation, then one fixed contact looks after you personally.
